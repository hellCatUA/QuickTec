Not random. React resets a form after its action runs. The fields that survive are the ones held in React state — title, the pickers, the dates. The ones that vanish are plain <input>s with nothing holding their value: Assignment ID, Ticket #, INC # and the whole scope of work. Every extra ticket row goes with them.

And one field is theatre. The Customer picker is not in the submitted schema at all. It filters the site list, and the job's customer is taken from whichever site you chose. It looks like data entry and is a search box.
