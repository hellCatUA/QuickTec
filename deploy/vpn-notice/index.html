Turn on the VPN to open QuickTec

QuickTec is not on the public internet. It runs on the company network, and that is on purpose — job details, customer sites, photos and pay never leave it.

  1. Open the VPN app on this phone or computer.
  2. Sign in with your work account if it asks.
  3. Switch it on, then come back here.

Already connected?

Give it a few seconds. This page opens QuickTec on its own the moment it can reach it — you do not have to keep trying.

No VPN on this device yet? Ask your supervisor or the office to set you up — it takes a couple of minutes and only has to be done once.
